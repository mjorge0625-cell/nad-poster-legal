Privacy Policy — NAD Poster

Last updated: October 6, 2026

NAD Poster is a personal automation tool built and operated by its owner to publish short-form videos to the owner's own TikTok account via TikTok's Content Posting API.

Information we access

  • TikTok account basics (via the user.info.basic scope): username and avatar, used only to confirm which account content is published to.
  • Videos you choose to publish (via the video.publish scope): video files you supply are uploaded to TikTok on your behalf, labeled as AI-generated where applicable.

How information is used

Accessed information is used solely to authenticate with TikTok and publish content you have explicitly selected. Nothing is used for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

Storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored securely on the operator's private server and are never shared with third parties.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any third party. The only data transmitted externally goes directly to TikTok's official APIs as required to perform the publishing actions you request.

Data retention & deletion

Tokens are retained only while the automation is in use. You may revoke the app's access at any time from your TikTok account settings, which immediately invalidates all tokens.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the app operator through the TikTok developer portal listing.
